# FORMAE by Milena — Website Plan

## Experience
- Build one polished, single-page portfolio experience with sticky section navigation and a BG/EN language switcher.
- Lead with a full-width image of a stylish, attainable apartment interior, concise positioning, and direct contact and portfolio actions.
- Keep the visual language architectural and editorial: warm light canvas, espresso type, sage accents, restrained motion, and generous spacing. Imagery should feature realistic mid-range apartments rather than luxury villas or extravagant homes.

## Sections
1. Hero with bilingual headline, introduction, and two actions.
2. About FORMAE and founder introduction.
3. Four services, with 3D visualization clearly emphasized.
4. Four-step process timeline.
5. Filterable portfolio gallery using custom interior imagery and upload-ready data entries.
6. Editorial philosophy quote.
7. Replaceable testimonial placeholders.
8. High-conversion contact area with phone, email, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Viber.
9. Minimal branded footer plus persistent WhatsApp and Viber actions.

## Technical details
- Keep content in structured bilingual data so future text and portfolio items are easy to replace.
- Use accessible controls, keyboard-friendly filters, semantic headings, mobile-first layouts, reduced-motion support, and optimized image dimensions.
- Add route-specific search and social metadata, brand fonts, a custom favicon, and smooth in-page navigation.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile widths, including language switching and portfolio filtering.
